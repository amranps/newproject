Founded in 2021,
CERAMINGLE has grown into a trusted and recognized brand in the premium drinkware industry,
dedicated to enhancing everyday coffee and tea experiences through beautifully crafted ceramic mugs.

With a mission to provide affordable,
high-quality products for students, working professionals,
and coffee enthusiasts alike, the company combines style, durability,
and functionality in every design.
Each CERAMINGLE mug is carefully manufactured using premium ceramic materials,
ensuring long-lasting performance while remaining both microwave-safe and dishwasher-safe for everyday convenience.
The brand offers a diverse collection of 25 unique mug designs,
featuring a variety of colors, patterns, and artistic styles that cater to different tastes and personalities.
Since its establishment, CERAMINGLE has achieved remarkable growth,
selling more than 15,000 mugs and earning the trust of over 7,500 satisfied customers.
Its dedication to quality and customer satisfaction is reflected in an outstanding average customer rating of 4.8 out of 5
and an impressive 95% customer satisfaction rate. Beyond its product success, CERAMINGLE has built a vibrant
and engaged community of over 10,000 social media followers who share a passion for coffee culture and creative design.
The company’s products are now available in 12 countries, demonstrating its expanding global presence and growing reputation among coffee and tea lovers worldwide.
By continuously introducing innovative designs, maintaining exceptional product standards, and prioritizing customer needs,
CERAMINGLE remains committed to delivering stylish, reliable, and enjoyable drinkware solutions while steadily expanding its reach and influence year after year.
